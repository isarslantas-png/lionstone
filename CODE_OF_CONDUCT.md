# Code of Conduct

We want Lionstone to be a welcoming project for editors, creators and developers of every background and experience level.

- Be kind and assume good intent. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- No harassment, discrimination, sexualised language or personal attacks.
- Respect people's time: give context, share reproducible examples.
- Respect privacy: never post someone's footage or personal data without consent.

Maintainers may remove content and block participants who break these rules. Report problems privately to the maintainer via the contact on the GitHub profile; reports are handled confidentially.

Adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
